What happens when delivery fails?

The backend schedules events against a virtual clock. Transient errors and timeouts retry with exponential backoff and seeded jitter. A 429 uses a simulated 3-second Retry-After. Other 4xx responses go directly to the dead-letter queue.

A timeout can happen after the receiver already committed its side effect. Retry then causes duplicate processing unless the receiver remembers the event ID. DeliveryLab models that distinction explicitly.

Redrive reruns the experiment with a recovery phase: dead letters get one successful attempt after the receiver is repaired. This is an educational simulator, not a background delivery service. No external requests or production database transactions are performed.
